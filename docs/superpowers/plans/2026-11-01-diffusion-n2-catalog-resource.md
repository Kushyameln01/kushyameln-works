- Preserve old canonical catalog, manager adoption, N16 fixture, native runtime/kernel/build and old receipts. A new preview is not canonical registration or management adoption.
- The N2 owner proposal currently lacks common record/rule/norm mapping; add those as separate entries with complete fixed source commits, independently anchored gates and evidence.
